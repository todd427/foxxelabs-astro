▸ What’s a Q-sort? (optional)

Q-methodology is a research technique developed by William Stephenson in 1935. Instead of rating each statement on its own scale — which lets you say "I agree with everything" — you have to commit to which statements you agree with most strongly, and which bother you most.

That forced commitment is what reveals distinct viewpoints in a group. It’s how cyberpsychologists, political scientists, and policy researchers find the actual disagreement underneath the noise.
