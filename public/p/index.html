Phones in Schools

Sort 30 statements onto a grid — from disagree on the left to agree on the right.

Anonymous · ~5 minutes · works on any device

How it works

Tap a card to pick it up — it shows up at the top.

Tap a column to drop it. Each column has a fixed number of slots: 2·4·5·8·5·4·2. The middle column ("Neutral") holds the most. The forcing is the point — you can’t put everything at +1.

When all 30 are placed, hit Submit. You’ll see which of five viewpoints your sort most resembles, and your contribution joins the live tally.

No personal data collected. Sort positions only.
