Phones in Schools

A Q-sort on mobile devices in education

What is this?

A Q-sort is a research method for finding the distinct viewpoints people hold on a contested topic. Instead of rating each statement on a 1-to-5 scale in isolation, you arrange all 30 statements relative to each other on a forced grid — committing to which agreements you hold most strongly and which disagreements bother you most.

How it works:

  1. Drag each of the 30 statements into one of seven columns, from strongly disagree (left) to strongly agree (right).
  2. Each column has a fixed number of slots. The forcing is the point — it surfaces what you actually prioritise.
  3. When all 30 are placed, submit your sort. You’ll see which of five viewpoints your sort most resembles, and your contribution joins a live tally.

Allow about 8 minutes. Works on phone, tablet, or desktop.

This page tallies how visitors arrange these statements. No personal information is collected. Your individual sort is not stored as a profile — only its contribution to the running totals (which statement landed at which position, with a date stamp).
