Fifty years of shipping software, the last eighteen months building AI systems end to end. I help organisations work out what AI will actually do for them, what to run on their own hardware, and where it will let them down.

What I advise on

AI adoption for small and mid-sized organisations. Where to start, what to build versus buy, what can run on-premises with EU-hosted or open models, and how to measure whether it is working.
The human side of AI. MSc Cyberpsychology (2026) and ongoing research on how people work with AI. Training staff to spot, and verify, the ways AI output goes wrong.
Technical leadership and delivery. Teams, schedules and technical due diligence, from a career of shipping to deadline.
