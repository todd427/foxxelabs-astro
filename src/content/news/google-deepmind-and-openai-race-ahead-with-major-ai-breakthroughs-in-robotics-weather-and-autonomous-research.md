## Google DeepMind Releases Suite of Advanced AI Systems

Google DeepMind has published three significant AI systems across genomics, weather forecasting, and robotics, marking a major acceleration in applied AI research.

### Genomics and Weather Frontiers

On September 1, 2026, Google DeepMind published **AlphaGenome Atlas**, described as a high-resolution map of human DNA. The same date saw Google DeepMind and Google Research jointly publish **WeatherNext 3**, their most advanced and accurate global weather AI model.

### Robotics Advances

Google DeepMind has made steady progress in embodied AI. In July 2026, the company published **Gemini Robotics 2**, described as bringing whole-body intelligence to robots. Earlier, in April 2026, Google DeepMind published **Gemini Robotics ER 1.6**, which powers real-world robotics tasks through enhanced embodied reasoning.

### Multi-Agent Research Collaboration

Two joint initiatives launched in May 2026 represent a shift toward AI-assisted scientific discovery. Google DeepMind, Google Cloud, Google Labs, and Google Research jointly published **Co-Scientist**, described as a multi-agent AI partner to accelerate research. On the same date, Google Labs, Google DeepMind, and Google Research jointly published **Gemini for Science**, described as AI experiments and tools for a new era of discovery.

## OpenAI Reaches Automated Research Intern Milestone

OpenAI stated it reached its goal of having an automated 'research intern' by September 2026—a system able to handle well-defined tasks that would otherwise take a skilled researcher several days.

The achievement reflects unprecedented scaling in AI-accelerated research. By mid-August 2026, the median OpenAI researcher was spending more than $600 a day on coding-agent inference at API prices, with the top 10% of researchers spending more than $7,000 in tokens daily on coding-agent inference.

OpenAI's research organization logs 3.1 agent-workdays of effort for every single human workday, a threshold crossed sometime before June 2026. Every category of OpenAI research activity—including writing code, running training evaluations, and monitoring live experiments—grew between January and August 2026.

### Security Incident

A security breach involving OpenAI's own coding agents forced researchers to pause parts of their AI-accelerated research work over the summer of 2026.
