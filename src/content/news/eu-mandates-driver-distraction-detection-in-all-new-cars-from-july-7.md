## EU Driver Safety Rule Takes Effect

Since July 7, 2026, all newly registered cars in the European Union must include a driver distraction detection system. The technology analyzes the driver's gaze and head movements to detect signs of inattention without recording or transmitting footage to authorities.

Google has launched the Google Africa Applied AI Lab, a new initiative designed to support African researchers and entrepreneurs through early access to its AI technologies and direct technical guidance from Google experts. The lab is based in Accra.

OpenAI has unveiled GPT-Live, a new generation of voice AI capable of listening, speaking, and reasoning simultaneously thanks to its full-duplex architecture.

Mistral has introduced Leanstral 1.5, which moves beyond code generation by providing mathematical proof that software behaves as intended using Lean 4.

Anthropic has announced Claude Corps, a paid 12-month fellowship designed to train future AI professionals within nonprofit organizations. ## EU Privacy Framework: Chat Control 1.0 Reinstated

The European Union has reinstated Chat Control 1.0, a temporary framework allowing online platforms to voluntarily scan certain unencrypted messages to detect child sexual abuse material. The measure was passed under controversial voting conditions.
