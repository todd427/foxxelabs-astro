## Modern LLMs Reason by Default

According to HackerNoon, modern LLM models reason by default, making chain-of-thought trick prompting less necessary than in earlier generations. This represents a significant shift in how developers should approach working with these systems.

## Context Engineering Emerges as the New Frontier

Context engineering—inputting information from database summaries, retrieval pipelines, and contextual memory—is described as the successor approach to traditional prompt engineering for LLMs. Rather than carefully crafting prompts, the focus is moving toward providing richer contextual information to models that can already reason effectively on their own.

## Industry Debate Reflects Broader Shift

The HackerNoon article cites an OpenAI community thread as a source for the claim that "prompt engineering is dead and context engineering is already obsolete," suggesting the field is actively debating what comes next for LLM optimization strategies.
