## Irish DPC Forces X to Suspend Grok Bot AI Training

The Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) has secured a suspension by platform X of the use of data collected by its AI agent Grok Bot to train AI models.

The Grok Bot AI agent began collecting so-called third-party data in late October 2026 to train X's AI systems. Following this development, DPC Commissioner Dale Sunderland stated that the DPC asked X to stop processing the Grok Bot-collected data. X confirmed to Politico Europe that it had complied with the DPC's request to stop processing Grok Bot data for AI training.

## Regulatory Scrutiny Extends Beyond X

The DPC is in talks with a second, unnamed technology company about its plans to use data collected by AI agents for model training.

DPC Commissioner Sunderland stated that the operation of AI agents itself is not a problem, but supervisory authorities must examine the lawfulness of the subsequent use of agent-collected data to train models.

## Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen with New Orchestrator Feature

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, 2026, expanding its Gemini model family with a system designed for rapid execution.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen is designed for rapid execution, while Carbon and Argon are aimed at harder tasks that need extended reasoning. Gemini 4 Hydrogen can handle coding and agent tasks on its own, or work alongside Gemini 4 Carbon.

### Orchestrator Feature and Performance

In Antigravity, Google's Orchestrator feature can switch between models during a session instead of locking a task to one model. Google says Antigravity with model switching came close to Carbon's task success on Terminal-Bench 4.0, using 24 percent less quota and finishing 19 percent faster.

On Terminal-Bench 4.1, Gemini 4 Hydrogen scored 57 percent, versus 18.2 percent for Gemini 3.8 Flash, according to Google's own benchmarks.

### Pricing and Configuration

Gemini 4 Hydrogen API pricing is set at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen users can set reasoning to low, medium, high, or xhigh; high is the default.

### Availability

The Gemini 4 Hydrogen rollout includes developer tools, business products, the Gemini app, and Search. Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available in the Gemini API and Android Studio's paid tier; business and consumer access varies by product and subscription.
