## Patent Issued for Portfolio-Level Credit Analysis

2GenPen Inc. was issued U.S. Patent No. 12,784,096 on November 16, 2026, for "Machine Learning Models for Qualitative Domains." The patent names co-founders Bailu Pan and Laurence H. Wadler as inventors.

This second patent expands IP protection to portfolio-level analysis, employing a two-stage neural network architecture capable of evaluating diverse portfolios with varying numbers of financial assets and generating unified credit risk indicators.

## Building on Earlier Innovation

2GenPen's first U.S. patent, No. 12,205,164, was issued on January 21, 2025, and established a machine learning framework integrating qualitative and quantitative information for financial risk assessment.

## Cross-Asset Credit Intelligence Framework

2GenPen's MUseQ® framework expresses cross-asset credit intelligence through MQval™, a continuous measure of relative credit survivability. In illustrative analysis, senior notes of a consumer asset-backed securitization scored 88.7 on the MQval™ scale, while general obligation bonds of a major U.S. city scored 85.6.

## Early Adoption and Leadership Credentials

Sisung Group, a New Orleans-based firm managing approximately $2 billion across a wide range of asset classes, is an early user of 2GenPen's technology.

2GenPen CEO Laurence H. Wadler brings a 30-year career spanning more than $500 billion in transactions across structured, municipal, and capital markets finance. He serves as an Adjunct Professor at New York University's Robert F. Wagner Graduate School of Public Service, where he has taught structured finance for 13 years, and was named Adjunct Professor of the Year at NYU Wagner in 2023.
