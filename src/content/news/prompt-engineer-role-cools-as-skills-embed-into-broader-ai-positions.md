## Standalone Role Cooling, Skills Expanding

The standalone 'prompt engineer' job title has cooled since the 2023–2024 hype, but the skill is now embedded in broader AI roles such as AI engineer, applied ML engineer, AI solutions consultant, and conversational designer.

## Salary Range Reflects Maturation

Compensation for prompt engineering work varies significantly by experience level. Coursera reports a US median salary of approximately $126,000 per year for prompt engineering roles, while ZipRecruiter shows entry-level prompt engineering postings closer to $63,000 per year in the US.

## Reasoning Models Change the Game

With the rise of reasoning models including OpenAI's o-series, Anthropic's extended thinking in Claude, and DeepSeek's R1, the need for nuanced prompts has shifted because these models now do much of the step-by-step reasoning internally.

## Agentic Systems Expand Automation Scope

Agentic systems such as Claude's computer use and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) ecosystem have expanded what well-crafted prompts can automate end-to-end.
