## OpenAI's Coding Agents Show Dramatic Capability Jump

OpenAI's internal coding agents have significantly improved their ability to work autonomously on research tasks. The agents completed 46% of research tasks estimated to take a human two to four hours without human help, up from 23% in March 2026. For longer tasks—those estimated at four to eight hours—the agents completed 35%, more than doubling their March 2026 performance of 13%.

Capabilities vary sharply by task type. The agents succeeded without human help on 79% of tasks monitoring training runs, but only 25% of tasks writing code for inference and serving stacks, illustrating what OpenAI describes as a "jagged frontier" of uneven capability.

## Measuring the Economic Impact

OpenAI's agent use has become substantial within the organization. The API-equivalent value of OpenAI agent use in September 2026 reached approximately twice its researchers' non-equity compensation. By October 2026, median agent use reached approximately $2,400 per researcher per day, measured at API list prices.

Despite this intensity of use, internal agent deployment takes only 2% of total research compute.

## Safety & Alignment Workweeks Grow 77%

Documented researcher workweeks in Safety & Alignment at OpenAI grew 77% from January to September 2026—the largest absolute increase of any research area. This growth occurs as overall research productivity accelerates through agent use.

## OpenAI's Policy Push on Transparency

OpenAI stated it believes AI labs should be required to track and publicly report indicators of recursive self-improvement (RSI). The company also says it wants to add reporting on differential acceleration of capabilities versus safety research—a direct response to concerns about whether safety work is keeping pace with capability gains.

## DeepMind's AlphaProof Code Advances Verification

In related progress, verification work was performed using AlphaProof Code, described as a novel agentic framework for formal verification that combines human expertise with AI agents. Signal CTO Ehren Kret stated: "Using AI agents to translate live code into valid Lean theorems is a major breakthrough."

DeepMind is eyeing ways to integrate AlphaProof Code verification technology into AI coding agents and software development workflows.
