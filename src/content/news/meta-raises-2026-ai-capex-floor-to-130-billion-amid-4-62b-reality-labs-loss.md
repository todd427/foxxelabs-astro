## Meta Raises AI Investment Floor to $130 Billion

Meta has raised its 2026 AI capital expenditure floor to $130 billion, underscoring the company's commitment to scaling its AI infrastructure despite ongoing losses in other divisions. The company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $60.8 billion.

However, Meta's Reality Labs division reported a $4.62 billion loss in the same quarter, continuing a pattern of significant investment in its metaverse efforts.

## Microsoft Copilot Reaches 30 Million Paid Seats

Microsoft's Copilot has reached 30 million paid seats, marking continued commercial traction for the AI assistant offering.

## Microsoft's Mixed Portfolio Performance

Microsoft's investments in generative AI have shown divergent results in Q4. Its investment in Anthropic gained $3.2 billion, while the company marked down its OpenAI investment by $600 million.

## Brookfield and NextEra Plan $100 Billion AI Campus in Kentucky

Brookfield and NextEra have announced plans for a $100 billion AI campus in Paducah, Kentucky, sited on a former US Department of Energy uranium facility. The campus is planned to reach 1.2 GW of compute capacity by 2032.
