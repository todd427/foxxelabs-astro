## Summer Submission Break Announced

The Cyberpsychology: Journal of Psychosocial Research on Cyberspace is introducing a summer break for new submissions from June 16 to August 31, 2026. New submissions will resume on September 1, 2026.

The break follows a sharp increase in submissions to the journal during 2025, reflecting growing interest in cyberpsychology research across Europe and beyond.

## Volume 20, Issue 1 Published January 28, 2026

The journal's first 2026 issue (Volume 20, Issue 1) was published on January 28, 2026. The issue includes articles on adolescents' and young people's mobile and social network habits, prosocial and antisocial online behaviours, social media influencers, and qualitative investigation of ChatGPT.

## Volume 20, Issue 2 Published April 14, 2026

The spring 2026 issue (Volume 20, Issue 2) was published on April 14, 2026. This issue contains eight articles covering topics including AI aversion, online sexual health knowledge, online dating, and videoconference fatigue.
