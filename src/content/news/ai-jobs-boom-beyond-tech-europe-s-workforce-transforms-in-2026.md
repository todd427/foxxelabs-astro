## AI Work Use Reaches 15% Across EU

Generative AI has moved from niche technology adoption to mainstream workforce practice across Europe. According to data from Eurostat, 15% of people aged 16 to 74 in the EU used generative AI for work in 2025.

## AI-Labelled Job Titles Surge Across Major European Markets

The expansion of AI-focused roles has been striking across Europe's largest economies. As of Q1 2026, AI-labelled job titles have grown substantially:

- Germany: 288 titles (up from 72 in Q1 2022)
- UK: 160 titles (up from 61 in Q1 2022)
- France: 138 titles (up from 35 in Q1 2022)
- Netherlands: 84 titles (up from 21 in Q1 2022)
- Spain: 81 titles (up from 8 in Q1 2022)

In 2022, all figures for AI-labelled job titles were below 1% in every country examined.

## AI Jobs Now Represent Significant Share of Total Vacancies

AI-labelled positions have grown from negligible to measurable shares of each country's job market:

- Germany leads at 4.2% of all job titles (up from 0.8% four years ago)
- France: 3.3% of all job titles
- UK: 2.7% of all job titles
- Spain: 2.3% of all job titles
- Netherlands: 2.2% of all job titles

## The Great Shift: AI Jobs Moving Outside Tech

Perhaps the most significant trend is where these AI roles are emerging. AI tools adoption has spread from software and data jobs to sales, HR, legal services, customer support and administrative roles.

This shift is evident across all major markets:

- Germany: 59% of AI-labelled jobs now sit outside tech occupations
- Netherlands: 58% outside tech
- France: 54% outside tech
- UK: 54% outside tech
- Spain: 36% outside tech (with 64% remaining in tech occupations)

## Methodology Note

These figures come from Indeed's job posting analysis. The platform classifies a job title as 'AI-labelled' when at least five postings under that label have AI in the employer's job description in a given calendar quarter.
