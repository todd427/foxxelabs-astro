## A New Framework for the Promptware Crisis

Researchers from Tsinghua University, Nanyang Technological University, Peking University, and King's College London have jointly authored a paper proposing 'promptware engineering' as a new methodology applying Software Engineering principles to prompt development.

The researchers identify what they term a 'promptware crisis': in practice, prompt engineering often relies on ad hoc, experimental approaches. Existing studies show it is largely trial-and-error, time-consuming, and challenging, with even experienced engineers facing difficulties.

## Understanding Promptware

OpenAI defines prompt engineering as 'designing and optimizing input prompts to effectively guide a language model's responses', whilst Meta defines it as 'a technique used in natural language processing to improve the performance of the language model by providing more context and information about the task at hand'.

The paper identifies 10 unique structural characteristics distinguishing promptware from traditional software, arising from the use of natural language and LLMs as runtime environments.

## A 27-Point Research Roadmap

The promptware engineering paper proposes a roadmap of 27 discrete research opportunities spanning prompt requirements engineering, design, implementation, testing, debugging, evolution, deployment, and monitoring.

## The Scale of LLM Integration

The paper states that LLMs such as GPT, LLaMA, and DeepSeek are increasingly integrated into software products by major companies including Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Apple, reaching millions of users.

A study by Tafreshipour et al. (MSR 2025) found that only 21.9% of prompt changes in real-world software repositories are documented. A survey by Wang et al. on LLMs in software testing found that about two-thirds of studies in that domain employ prompt engineering.

## Growing Market and Workforce Demand

LinkedIn data cited by Refonte Learning shows a 250% increase in job postings for roles related to prompt engineering in one year (by 2025). Industry research cited by Refonte Learning projects the prompt engineering market to grow at approximately 33% annually through 2030.

Refonte Learning characterises prompt engineering as a skill now embedded within other roles such as AI Developer, NLP Specialist, Data Scientist, Product Manager, or Conversational Designer, rather than primarily a standalone job title.

## Emerging Orchestration Tools

LangChain and Microsoft's PromptFlow are identified as leading orchestration frameworks enabling engineers to chain multiple prompts together and interface with external data or APIs as part of a pipeline.
