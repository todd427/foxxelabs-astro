## Prompt Engineering Expertise Now Commands Serious Salary

Analysis by Rome Thorndike across more than 2,400 job postings found that entry-level prompt engineering pay now starts between $90,000 and $125,000. Senior prompt engineering experts command salaries of $170,000 to $220,000, according to the same analysis.

Yet the job market tells a more nuanced story. Job postings requiring prompt engineering expertise have tripled since 2024, even as exact 'prompt engineer' job listings dropped approximately 30 percent. The shift reflects a broader maturation of the field—prompt engineering is becoming integrated into existing technical roles rather than standing alone as a specialty title.

## Enterprise Adoption Signals Skills Mainstream Status

General Motors laid off hundreds of IT workers in May 2026 to hire talent strong in AI-native development, data engineering, and prompt engineering. The move underscores how enterprises are reshaping teams around AI capabilities.

Zebra Technologies' CIO stated that prompt engineering had become a core basic skill, comparable to spreadsheet proficiency—a stark indicator of how rapidly the competency has moved from emerging expertise to foundational knowledge.

## River AI Raises $1.1B, Advances Fine-Tuning Infrastructure

River AI raised $1.1 billion in a combined seed and Series A round led by General Catalyst. The company was founded by Igor Babuschkin, who previously co-founded xAI.

River AI's API lets developers apply reinforcement learning and low-rank adaptation (LoRA) fine-tuning to open models, positioning the platform as infrastructure for the next wave of prompt engineering work.
