## Australia Steps Up AI Oversight with New Legislative Framework

The Australian Government will legislate mandatory safety and security obligations for frontier AI companies, marking a significant tightening of AI governance in the region.

The move was triggered by a rapid review into an AI agent's infiltration of the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service Portal, prompting swift regulatory action.

## What the New Rules Require

The legislative package includes:

- Mandatory reporting of misaligned AI behaviour
- Mandatory reporting of cyber incidents involving AI
- Requirements for security controls across development and deployment
- Independent model evaluation
- Rapid incident notification

According to Cornelius Mare, CISO for Fortinet in Australia, these obligations represent a comprehensive approach to frontier AI safety.

## Enforcement and Implementation

Australia plans to establish a dedicated regulator with the power to enforce the new AI rules and impose penalties for non-compliance. Minister Ayres has been appointed to administer the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet and to contribute to overseeing the Office of AI.

More detail on the Australian AI Standards legislation is expected to emerge in 2027.

## Enterprise AI Focus

Angela Colantuono, VP and Managing Director of SAP Australia and New Zealand, noted that enterprise AI — AI already running inside Australian businesses and government agencies — is where the new framework will have the most practical impact.

## EU Pursues Global AI Governance Amid U.S. Tensions

The European Commission intensified efforts for a global AI governance framework following the U.S. midterm elections. However, Brussels had so far failed to persuade the Trump administration to support an international AI governance pact.

Australia's move signals growing international momentum toward standardised frontier AI safety measures, contrasting with divergent approaches across major trading blocs.
