## Common Sandbox Rules Adopted

The European Commission adopted common rules on November 10, 2026 for every AI regulatory sandbox that EU countries must run under the AI Act. Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2487 was published in the Official Journal on November 11, 2026 and will take effect December 1, 2026.

## No Conformity Status, Full Liability Remains

Companies completing an EU AI regulatory sandbox receive written proof of completed activities and an exit report, but neither document has the status or legal effect of an EU declaration of conformity. Sandbox participants remain liable for third-party damage and stay responsible for complying with applicable EU law.

An exit report must reach the sandbox participant no later than 45 working days after participation ends.

## Support for SMEs and Start-ups

SMEs and start-ups receive priority access and free regulatory guidance, advice and supervision inside AI regulatory sandboxes, including supervision of real-world testing and support for conformity assessment.

## Member State Deadline Extended

Each EU Member State has until August 2, 2027 to have at least one national AI regulatory sandbox operational. The original EU AI Act deadline for national sandboxes was August 2, 2026; the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) moved it back one year to August 2, 2027.

When Parliament and the Council reached their provisional deal on the Digital Omnibus in May 2026, only one of the 27 EU Member States had an operational AI regulatory sandbox.

## Union-Level Sandbox Pathway

The EU AI Office may now run a Union-level sandbox for certain AI systems, with priority access for SMEs, start-ups and small mid-cap companies.

## Data Protection Oversight

The European Data Protection Supervisor submitted formal comments on the draft sandbox regulation on March 6, 2026.

## Attractiveness Questions

Deirdre Ahern, a law professor at Trinity College Dublin, argued in a peer-reviewed paper that the missing conformity status is one of several reasons AI sandboxes may struggle to attract providers.

## High-Risk AI Deadlines

High-risk AI obligations under the EU AI Act are due on December 2, 2027 for stand-alone systems and August 2, 2028 for AI embedded in regulated products.
