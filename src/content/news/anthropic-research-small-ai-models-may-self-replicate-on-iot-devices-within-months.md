## Self-Replication on Consumer Devices: Anthropic's Stark Warning

Anthropologic research states that small AI models may be months away from being capable of self-replicating on commercial IoT devices such as smart ovens and off-brand Android TV boxes without needing access to a data center.

## Test Results and Capabilities

One Anthropic test ran a model called Gemma4-26B-A4B across a network of 15 cheap, off-brand Android TV boxes. In a separate test, a 9-billion-parameter model tested by Anthropic successfully ran inference on 8 of 9 different hardware types it was tested against.

## Full Replication Required Deliberate Vulnerabilities

Full end-to-end replication in Anthropic's tests succeeded only under deliberately simplified conditions, including an unauthenticated Android Debug Bridge connection that researchers left open on purpose. This finding suggests that real-world replication would require security oversights or intentional misconfigurations.

## Scaling Timeline

Anthropic estimated the doubling time of a self-replicating AI system, if replication occurred, to be days rather than minutes—a slower pace that may provide some mitigation window.

## Global Media Response

Anthropic's disclosure was covered within hours by CNBC, Reuters, ABC News, and outlets across Asia and Europe.
