## Key Developments

OpenAI has made the surprising decision to shut down its Sora AI video generation app, just months after its high-profile launch. The closure has triggered the collapse of a $1 billion partnership deal with Disney, which had agreed to invest in OpenAI and license characters for use in Sora. No money had actually changed hands as the deal was never finalized.

The shutdown comes amid dramatic usage decline. Market intelligence firm Appfigures reports that Sora's US App Store downloads fell 32% month-on-month in December 2025 and dropped a further 45% in January 2026. OpenAI says it will reallocate resources toward robotics and autonomous software systems that can operate with minimal supervision.

## Industry Context

This development reflects a broader industry shift from consumer-facing AI applications toward enterprise solutions. The emergence of agentic AI systems - digital workers capable of autonomous task performance - is driving significant growth. Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise applications will use task-specific AI agents by 2026, up from less than 5% in 2025.

The timing coincides with OpenAI's release of GPT-5.4 in March 2026, featuring a one-million-token context window and native computer-use skills. The model achieved a 75% score on desktop productivity benchmarks, surpassing the 72.4% human baseline.

## Practical Implications

For Irish and European businesses, this signals that enterprise AI applications offer more sustainable value than consumer entertainment tools. The projected $2.52 trillion AI market in 2026 will be dominated by business productivity solutions rather than creative applications.

The industry is transitioning from "brute-force scaling to researching new architectures, from flashy demos to targeted deployments," according to TechCrunch analysis. European companies should focus on practical AI implementations that augment workforce capabilities rather than pursuing viral consumer applications.

## Open Questions

It remains unclear whether other video generation platforms will face similar market pressures, and how OpenAI's pivot to robotics will affect its competitive position against Google and Microsoft. The sustainability of consumer AI applications versus enterprise solutions continues to be tested in real market conditions.
