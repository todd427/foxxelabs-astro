## Microsoft and Mistral Partnership Strengthens European AI Ecosystem

Microsoft and Mistral announced a multibillion-dollar agreement on July 21, 2026, with Microsoft committing to leverage Mistral's expanded Europe-based GPU infrastructure to increase capacity for AI development and support Microsoft's cloud and AI services.

Mistral is deploying thousands of NVIDIA Vera Rubin GPUs across its European data centers to provide a shared platform for training, inference, and large-scale deployment.

## New Models Available on Microsoft Platforms

Mistral Medium 3.5 and OCR 4 models are now available in Microsoft Foundry, and Mistral Medium 3.5 is now available in Microsoft Copilot Studio.

Organizations can deploy Mistral models through Azure and Azure Local across cloud, cloud-connected, and fully disconnected environments while maintaining control over data and operations.

## EU Tightens Tech Competition Rules

On July 16, 2026, the European Commission issued two sets of binding specification measures to Google under the Digital Markets Act.

The first specification measure aims to ensure competitors' AI services can compete with Google's AI services by having equal access to features on Google's Android devices.

The second specification measure aims to rebalance the playing field by giving third-party search engines access to search data that only Google Search can collect at scale.
