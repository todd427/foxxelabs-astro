## ChatGPT Expands to WhatsApp in the EEA

ChatGPT is now available on WhatsApp for users in the European Economic Area, bringing advanced AI capabilities directly to one of Europe's most widely used messaging platforms.

The integration supports image, voice, and multilingual chat, making ChatGPT accessible across multiple interaction modes for EEA-based WhatsApp users.
