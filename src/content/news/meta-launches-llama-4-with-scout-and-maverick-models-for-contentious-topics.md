## Meta Advances AI Reasoning with Llama 4 Release

Meta has released Llama 4, featuring models named Scout and Maverick that are designed to handle politically and socially contentious questions more effectively than their predecessors.

## Ember Framework Enables Multi-Model AI Collaboration

Researchers from Nvidia, Google, and Foundry have introduced Ember, an open-source framework that facilitates interactions among different AI models with varying reasoning strengths and response times. This development enables more flexible deployment of AI systems across different use cases.

## Altair Accelerates Simulation with Geometric Deep Learning

Altair's HyperWorks® platform uses geometric deep learning to deliver simulation results up to 1,000 times faster than previous methods, representing a significant efficiency gain for computational engineering workflows.

## Exponential Growth in AI Training Resources

Computational resources used to train AI models have been increasing exponentially, with estimates indicating a 4–5x annual growth rate from 2010 to 2024. Supporting this trend, the size of datasets employed for training language models has grown approximately 2.9 times per year since 2010.
