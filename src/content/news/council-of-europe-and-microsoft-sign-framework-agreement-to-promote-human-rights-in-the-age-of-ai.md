## Framework Agreement Signed in Strasbourg

The Council of Europe and Microsoft signed a memorandum of understanding establishing a framework cooperation agreement on 6 October 2026 at the Council of Europe's headquarters in Strasbourg. The memorandum was signed by Council of Europe Secretary General Alain Berset and Microsoft Vice Chair and President Brad Smith.

## Goals and Mechanisms

The framework agreement aims to promote human rights, democracy, and the rule of law through responsible and inclusive technological development and to address challenges in the digital environment, including AI. The agreement includes dialogue through Microsoft's Open Innovation Centre in Strasbourg as a named mechanism for cooperation.

The agreement also supports initiatives that promote linguistic and cultural diversity, including regional or minority languages.

## Expanding Existing Cooperation

The memorandum extends existing cooperation to the Register of Damage for Ukraine, which Microsoft's AI for Good Lab already supports by applying AI and satellite analysis to assess damage.

Microsoft previously supported the Council of Europe's Budapest Convention on Cybercrime through the Global Project on Cybercrime and the Cybercrime Octopus project.
