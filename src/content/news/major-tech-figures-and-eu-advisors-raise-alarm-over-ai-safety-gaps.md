## Safety Concerns Shadow Anthropic's Path to Public Markets

The Center for AI Safety (CAIS) has co-signed a joint statement with prominent figures from tech and academia criticizing Anthropic for contradictions between its safety messaging and its research and development strategy. The statement was released ahead of Anthropic's planned initial public offering.

According to the joint statement, Anthropic warns that self-improving AI could lead to concentrated power and loss of human control while simultaneously racing to develop such technology. The statement also accuses Anthropic of lobbying for regulatory policies that favor itself.

Signatories include Harvard psychology professor Steven Pinker, MIT professor Max Tegmark, Hugging Face co-founder Thomas Wolf, and xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin.

## EU Advisors Issue Unprecedented Joint Call for Stronger AI Safeguards

In an unusual coordinated move, the European Commission's Group of Chief Scientific Advisors and the Group on Ethics in Science and New Technologies issued a joint statement on the next phase of AI on 16 November 2026. A combined statement from both groups on AI had not previously been issued.

The EU advisors recommend that the rate of AI development and deployment should be calibrated to degrees of potential danger. They also recommend that the EU must develop its own capacity to evaluate AI models, and avoid delegating this task to commercial developers.

The advisors warn that a lack of political commitment or institutional capacity risks undermining existing AI safety obligations. They further caution that external efforts to delay or weaken AI legislation could compromise safeguards.

The Group of Chief Scientific Advisors consists of seven highly qualified, specialised, independent experts appointed in their personal capacity to advise the European Commission.

## Regulatory Framework Under Review

The EU advisory statement references the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), the 2025 General-Purpose AI Code of Practice, and the Cloud and AI Development Act COM(2026) 502 final.
