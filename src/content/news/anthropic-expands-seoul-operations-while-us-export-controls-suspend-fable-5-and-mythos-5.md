## US Export Controls Suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5

The expansion comes shortly after the US government issued an export control directive on June 12, 2026, suspending all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5. The regulatory action adds complexity to Anthropic's international operations during a period of significant growth.
