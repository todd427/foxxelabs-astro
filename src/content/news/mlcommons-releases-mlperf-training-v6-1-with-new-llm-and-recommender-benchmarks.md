## MLPerf Training v6.1 Expands Benchmark Coverage

MLCommons announced new results for the MLPerf Training v6.1 benchmark suite on November 16, 2026. The latest release adds two new benchmarks: **DLRMv4-HSTU**, a recommender system benchmark, and **Qwen3.5-397B**, a large-scale LLM post-training workload.

### New LLM Post-Training Benchmark

Qwen3.5-397B marks a first-of-its-kind workload for MLPerf Training, using large-scale post-training via Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). The benchmark trains models to reason independently and solve software engineering tasks through tool-calling.

### New Recommender System Benchmark

DLRMv4-HSTU uses the HSTU attention-based architecture and trains on the Yambda dataset, described as one of the largest open-sourced recommendation datasets available.

## Participation and Hardware Trends

MLPerf Training v6.1 received submissions from 24 organizations, yielding 153 submitted systems and 250 test results. Cloud-based submissions showed significant growth, with 57 of the 153 systems (37%) running in the cloud, up from 29% in v6.0.

## First-Time Hardware and Submitter Appearances

The benchmark suite includes results using NVIDIA Rubin-generation GPUs for the first time, as well as results using Google's TPUv7 (Ironwood) for the first time. Crusoe also appears as a first-time MLPerf Training submitter in v6.1.
