## TensorX Secures €8M Seed Funding

TensorX, an Irish-founded sovereign AI company, has secured more than €8 million in seed funding. The company is helping organisations to run AI inferencing in Europe.

## Dell Technologies Partnership

TensorX has partnered with Dell Technologies to provide infrastructure for scaling its sovereign AI platform.

## Context: Ireland's AI Moment

The funding round comes as Ireland undertakes the Presidency of the Council of the EU and positions itself as a hub for trustworthy and sovereign AI development in Europe.
