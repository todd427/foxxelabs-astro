## Research Fund Now Open

The BPS Cyberpsychology Section Research Fund is currently accepting applications. The fund offers up to £500 per applicant for participant payments in cyberpsychology research.

**Application Deadline:** 30 September 2026

**Important Timeline:** Research Fund recipients must have the BPS invoiced by Friday 18 December 2026.

Membership of the BPS Cyberpsychology Section is restricted to members of the British Psychological Society.

## About the BPS Cyberpsychology Section

The Section was established in 2019 and operates a monthly scheduled Twitter chat called #CyberSectionChat, also established in 2019.

**Current Leadership:**
- Dr Linda K. Kaye, Associate Head in the Department of Psychology at Edge Hill University, is listed as Past Chair and a founding member of the Section
- Dr Lisa J. Orchard, Senior Lecturer in Psychology at the University of Wolverhampton, serves as Secretary
- Dr John Blythe, Industry Representative, is an honorary research fellow at UCL Dawes Centre for Future Crime
- The Chair Elect position is currently vacant

## Cyberpsychology Journal Updates

Cyberpsychology: Journal of Psychosocial Research on Cyberspace reopened its submission system on 1 September 2026 following a summer closure and has introduced updated submission requirements designed to strengthen quality, transparency, and reproducibility.

The journal published a new issue on 4 September 2026 containing 15 new articles covering topics including artificial intelligence and synthetic relationships, digital privacy, content moderation, media multitasking, and digital inclusion.
