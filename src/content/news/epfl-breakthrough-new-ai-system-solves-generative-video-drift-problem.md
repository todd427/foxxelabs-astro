## EPFL Tackles Generative Video Coherence Challenge

A team of EPFL researchers has developed a new AI system that addresses the problem of drift in generative video, which causes sequences to become incoherent after a handful of seconds. This breakthrough tackles one of the key limitations in current video generation technology, where longer sequences tend to lose visual consistency and narrative coherence.

## Wider AI Innovation Landscape

The EPFL development comes amid a broader wave of AI breakthroughs across multiple domains:

**Soft Robotics Adaptation**

Researchers have developed an AI control system that enables soft robotic arms to learn a wide repertoire of motions and tasks once, then adjust to new scenarios on the fly without needing retraining. This approach dramatically reduces the computational overhead traditionally required for robotic learning and adaptation.

**AI's Selective Job Impact**

Research by academics at King's College London and the AI Objectives Institute found that what matters for AI's impact on jobs is not just how much of a job AI can do, but which parts of that job it performs. This nuanced finding suggests that workforce displacement concerns may depend less on overall task automation capability and more on which specific job functions are affected.

**Battery Research Acceleration**

An agentic AI tool for battery researchers, developed at the University of Michigan, can predict the cycle life of new battery concepts using data from just 50 cycles. This capability substantially accelerates materials discovery and battery development timelines.

**3D Printing Quality Control**

A new AI system has been developed that can fix 3D printing defects in real time, addressing the limitation that the majority of 3D printers operate on open-loop systems. Real-time defect correction could significantly improve manufacturing yield rates across industries.

**Generative AI Adoption and Mindfulness**

A study surveying more than 440 project managers worldwide found a critical connection between mindfulness and the adoption of generative AI in the workplace. The finding highlights the human factors influencing technology adoption in professional environments.

## Understanding AI Information Processing

A study from the University at Albany found that AI systems may organise information in far more intricate ways than previously thought, revealing the geometry behind how AI agents learn. This research contributes to fundamental understanding of how neural networks represent and process information.

## Bio-Hybrid Memory Breakthroughs

Researchers combined synthetic DNA with a semiconductor to create an ultra-low-power memory device capable of storing and processing information in the same place. This bio-hybrid approach opens new possibilities for energy-efficient computing architectures.
