## Frontier Model Monitoring and Safeguard Testing

OpenAI published a post describing how it monitors frontier models, investigates concerning behavior, and tests the limits of its safeguards.

## Efficient Real-Time Policy Monitoring

OpenAI published research showing how it trains and deploys efficient monitors that use a model's internal activity to detect and interrupt policy-violating behavior in real time.

## Agent Behavior in Safety Evaluations

OpenAI published research studying agent behavior in safety evaluations and in production, exploring how models' interpretations of evaluation objectives relate to eval-production performance gaps.

## Chain-of-Thought Monitorability

OpenAI published research on how pretraining, architecture, reinforcement learning, and model-generated data affect two proxies for chain-of-thought monitorability. Additionally, OpenAI published research finding that reasoning models struggle to control their chains of thought, and characterised this limitation as reassuring for AI safety.

OpenAI open-sourced monitorability evaluation datasets, code, and an evaluation filtering strategy for studying monitorability.

## Monitoring Mechanisms and Task Reasoning

OpenAI published research finding that reasoning about how a task will be monitored or rewarded appears to draw on several overlapping processes, not a single mechanism.

## LASER Tool for Safety Data Identification

OpenAI introduced LASER (Logistic Augmented Sampling over Embeddings, Recursively), a tool that combines logistic classifiers with reasoning models to identify high-value data for safety evaluation.

## Red Teaming in High-Stakes Domains

OpenAI published research on improving model robustness in high-stakes domains such as cyber and biology using automated red teaming, noting that many tasks in those domains are dual-use and misuse can cause significant harm.

## Safety Case Guidelines

OpenAI published initial guidelines for safety cases covering technical safeguards, operational practices, and investigations of misalignment incidents during frontier AI training.

## Security Incident Analysis

OpenAI published findings from a Hugging Face security incident, covering model security, monitoring, and alignment implications.

## Safety Fellowship and External Funding

OpenAI introduced the OpenAI Safety Fellowship, described as a program supporting independent alignment and safety research.

OpenAI committed $7.5M to The Alignment Project to fund independent research developing mitigations to safety and security risks from misaligned AI.
