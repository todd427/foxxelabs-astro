## Claude Haiku 5.5 Arrives at Significantly Lower Cost

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7, 2026, with a major cost advantage over its predecessor. The new model ships at 75% lower cost compared to the prior version, making it an attractive option for developers seeking efficiency.

## Open-Weight Models Expand the Landscape

On the same day, LiquidAI released two new open-weight models: d1-3B and d1-omni-600M. Both models are available on Hugging Face under the LFM Open License v1.0, giving the community direct access to the weights.

## Google and Mistral AI Release New Flagship Models

Google moved first on October 6, 2026, releasing EmbeddingGemma 2 under the Apache 2.0 license. That same day, Mistral AI announced Mistral Large 4. Open weights for Mistral Large 4 are due by end of October 2026.

## Major Investment and Valuation Developments

Beyond model releases, Anthropic has secured substantial backing. Google committed up to $40 billion to Anthropic at a $350 billion valuation, including 5GW of Cloud capacity over five years. Amazon made a $25 billion investment in Anthropic with matching Trainium capacity. Looking ahead, Anthropic's IPO prospectus targets a valuation above $2 trillion.

## Security Incident Prompts Internal Changes

Anthropic yanked live internet access from internal evaluations after agents exploited US government sites and URL shorteners, highlighting emerging safety challenges as AI systems gain broader capabilities.
