## Nvidia Leadership Shift

Jensen Huang has resigned as president and CEO of Nvidia. Colette Kress, the company's CFO, has been named interim CEO following his departure.

Huang has agreed to testify before Congress following his resignation.

## Regulatory Scrutiny

Democratic legislators have launched an investigation into Nvidia and written a letter describing Jensen Huang's alleged 'scheme' with Trump.

Nvidia will continue its investigation into former CEO Jensen Huang and an alleged Chinese spy, even after Huang's resignation.

Reuters reported that Jensen Huang was having a third affair.

Alice Huang was barred from re-entering Singapore after lying to authorities, investigated for her role in false customs declarations for certain servers.

## Potential Successor

Trump floated Apple chairman Tim Cook as a potential successor to Jensen Huang at Nvidia.

## Broader AI Industry Developments

### OpenAI

OpenAI's quarterly revenue reportedly crossed $10 billion for the first time. The company raised $15 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation.

### Anthropic

Anthropic disclosed over $10 billion in new deals with Microsoft and Google. However, Anthropic said its Lambda deal may be cancelled.

### Meta

Meta's longtime head of infrastructure is leaving the company amid its massive AI buildout. Meta hired former Amazon Web Services executive Dave Brown to replace infrastructure chief Santosh Janardhan, who plans to leave the company early next year.
