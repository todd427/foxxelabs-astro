## IBM and Microsoft Launch Imagine Enterprise

IBM and Microsoft jointly launched Imagine Enterprise, a platform designed to support enterprise AI deployments. The platform is seeded with 6,000 pre-built agents tailored for sectors including finance and retail.

Imagine Enterprise integrates AI agents with governance tools and consulting services, positioning the offering as a comprehensive solution for organisations looking to deploy AI agents at scale.

## OpenAI Acquires Odessia, Shuts Down Service

OpenAI acquired Odessia, an AI travel agent startup founded by Francis Davidson. Following the acquisition, OpenAI shut down Odessia's service and Francis Davidson joined OpenAI.

## Funding Surge Across AI Agent Infrastructure

**Plurai** raised $13 million in seed funding to build simulation infrastructure for trustworthy, self-improving AI agents.

**Bay** raised $50 million in funding for AI agent security solutions.

**LangChain** raised $300 million in a funding round led by CapitalG. The company's post-round valuation is $3 billion, up from $1.25 billion in 2025.

## Fireworks AI Suffers Second Breach in Two Months

A cyberattack on Fireworks AI allowed hackers to access Notion customers' chat transcripts. The breach marks Fireworks AI's second data breach in as many months.

## Anthropic Releases Claude Mythos 5.2 with Looser Safeguards

Anthropric released Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9, 2026. The model is described as the same underlying model as Claude Fable 5.2, but with looser cyber and bio safeguards available to verified organisations.
