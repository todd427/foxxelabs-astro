## AI Emerges as Top Driver of Job Cuts in 2026

Artificial intelligence has become the leading single cause of job losses in the United States, according to data from Challenger, Gray & Christmas. Through September 2026, AI drove 120,136 US job cuts—representing 21% of all announced job losses year-to-date.

The broader employment picture shows some improvement compared to 2025. In September 2026 alone, US employers announced 43,281 job cuts, down 20% from 54,064 in September 2025. Year-to-date through September 2026, total US job cuts reached 573,195, a significant 39% decline from the 946,426 cuts announced in the same period of 2025.

## McKinsey's Workforce Transition Challenge

Despite the year-over-year improvement in layoff numbers, McKinsey's research paints a sobering picture for displaced workers. According to the firm's report "Workforce in motion: Skills and pathways to future jobs in the United States," roughly 11 million workers in declining occupations may need to transition to new jobs due to AI. The range could extend from 6 million to 16 million workers, depending on the pace of automation adoption and its impact on labour demand.

McKinsey estimates that almost half of the roughly 11 million workers facing AI-driven job transitions face an "unpaved path" to re-employment. The occupations most affected among those with difficult transition prospects are office and administrative support, retail and sales, and transportation and logistics. Overall, McKinsey estimates that 70% of workers displaced by AI will need to reinvent themselves professionally.

## Amazon's Long-Term Automation Plans

Looking further ahead, Amazon plans to eliminate up to 500,000 jobs by 2033, with the expectation that its warehouses might eventually have no workers at all, as previously reported by The New York Times.
