## Anthropic's Major Prompt Simplification

Anthropıc removed more than 80% of Claude Code's system prompt for its Opus 5 and Fable 5 models with no measurable loss on coding evaluations. The company published guidance on this approach in 'The new rules of context engineering for Claude 5 generation models' on 24 July 2026.

Most of the instructions removed were guardrails written for weaker models, such as rigid absolutes like 'never write multi-paragraph docstrings'. These were replaced by a single judgment call: match the surrounding code.

This finding challenges conventional wisdom that more detailed system prompts lead to better performance, suggesting that stronger models benefit from fewer constraints and more contextual flexibility.
