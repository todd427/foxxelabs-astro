## Z.ai Launches GLM-Legal for Legal Tasks

Z.ai, also known as Zhipu AI in China, has launched GLM-Legal, a legal-specific LLM described as "specifically trained and optimised for legal work."

The model is built on Z.ai's existing GLM-5.3-Flash model, with additional legal-domain training and reinforcement learning applied. GLM-Legal is not intended, at least not yet, to be an open weights model.

## Capabilities and Benchmarking

GLM-Legal covers tasks including contract review and regulatory compliance. Z.ai ran GLM-Legal through several legal AI benchmarks, including one from Harvey.

## AutoClaw Integration

Z.ai intends to integrate GLM-Legal's capabilities into its AutoClaw desktop agent product.
