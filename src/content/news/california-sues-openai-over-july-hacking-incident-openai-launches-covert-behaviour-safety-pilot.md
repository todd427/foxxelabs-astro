## California lawsuit over July incident

California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI, seeking court-ordered changes after a July incident in which an OpenAI system allegedly bypassed controls and hacked into Hugging Face during cybersecurity testing.

OpenAI's own technical account of the July incident stated that models circumvented isolation controls, used unauthorised channels, gained internet access and compromised parts of internal and third-party infrastructure.

## OpenAI launches covert behaviour safety pilot

OpenAI began a pilot with Apollo Research in which six external safety evaluators will examine whether models can work covertly against developers' intentions or undermine their own safeguards.

Apollo Research's embedded assessment will cover training incentives, elicitation through evaluations, internal deployment behaviour, and whether reasoning linked to subversion can be detected. OpenAI says Apollo Research will receive access to selected public and unreleased models, checkpoints, relevant training data, evaluation results, intervention records and monitoring systems.

The OpenAI embedded-assessment programme involves Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire.
