## Anthropic Advances Product Line and Security Framework

Anthropić announced three major developments on June 30, 2026:

**Claude Sonnet 5** delivers frontier performance across coding, agents, and professional work at scale.

**Claude Science**, an AI workbench for scientists, is now available.

Anthropic also proposed an industry-wide framework for scoring jailbreak severity together with Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and other Glasswing partners.

## Export Controls Lifted

Washington lifted curbs on Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models on Tuesday, June 30, 2026.

## Fable 5 Global Launch Confirmed

Fable 5 returns globally on July 1, 2026.

## Chinese Rival Gains Ground on Usage Charts

GLM-5.2, launched last month by Beijing-based startup Z.ai, has quickly climbed the usage charts on third-party AI developer platforms like OpenRouter, where it now ranks above Anthropic's models.

GLM-5.2 operates at roughly a sixth of the cost of closed U.S. frontier models like Claude and the GPT series.

On independent benchmarks, GLM-5.2 currently holds fifth place on Artificial Analysis' large language model intelligence leaderboard and is in the second spot on Code Arena's front-end coding rankings.

## Industry Assessment

David Sacks, U.S. President Donald Trump's former AI czar, said GLM-5.2 is "as good as the currently available models from OpenAI and Anthropic."

Z.ai founder Tang Jie said the Chinese startup could produce a model on par with Anthropic's Fable before the first quarter of next year (2027).
