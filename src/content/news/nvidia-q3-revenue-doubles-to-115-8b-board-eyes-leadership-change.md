## Nvidia Delivers Record Q3 Results

Nvidia has reported Q3 revenue of $115.8B, representing a 103% year-over-year increase. The company's Data Center segment alone generated $107.6B in Q3 revenue, underscoring the sustained demand for AI infrastructure. Looking ahead, Nvidia has guided Q4 revenue to $130B.

## Nvidia Board Explores Leadership Transition

In a significant development, Nvidia's board has approached Arm's CEO about replacing Jensen Huang as CEO.

## OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra at Lower Price Point

OpenAI has shipped GPT-6.2 Astra, priced at $8/$40 per million tokens—20% below GPT-6 Astra pricing.

## Federal Reserve Flags AI as Growing Financial Risk

87% of Federal Reserve market contacts have named AI as a risk that could shake the financial system, up from 50% in the spring.
