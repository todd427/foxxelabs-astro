## ChatGPT's Market Share Falls Below 50% for First Time

OpenAI's ChatGPT dropped to 46.4% of AI assistant users in May 2026, marking a significant milestone—the first time the market-leading platform has fallen below the 50% threshold. The shift underscores the increasingly competitive landscape in generative AI, with alternative AI assistants gaining ground among users.

## Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques Reshape API Efficiency

Developers working with AI APIs in 2026 now face a more complex billing landscape. API responses separate visible content tokens from billed reasoning_tokens, which remain hidden in the output. This distinction has material cost implications: a single 'High Effort' reasoning API call can consume 10 times the tokens of the final visible output, making token efficiency a critical consideration for production deployments.

## Chain-of-Symbol Emerges as Superior Strategy for Complex Tasks

Chain-of-Symbol (CoS) outperforms Chain-of-Thought (CoT) on spatial reasoning, game states, and structured planning tasks, offering developers a more effective approach for these problem domains.

## Few-Shot Learning: Finding the Sweet Spot

The optimal number of few-shot examples for production prompts typically falls between 2 and 5. Fewer than two examples often fails to establish a clear pattern, while more than five rarely improves performance—a finding that can help teams optimize both cost and accuracy in real-world implementations.
