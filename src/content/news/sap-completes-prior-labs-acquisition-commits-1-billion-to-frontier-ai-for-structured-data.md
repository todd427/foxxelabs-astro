## SAP Completes Prior Labs Acquisition

SAP has completed the acquisition of Prior Labs, the pioneer of Tabular Foundation Models (TFMs). The enterprise software giant is committing to investing more than €1 billion over the next four years to scale Prior Labs into a globally leading frontier AI lab for the structured data that underpins the world's businesses.

## Independence Maintained

Prior Labs will continue to operate as an independent entity within SAP, preserving the research lab's autonomy while benefiting from SAP's resources and reach.

## Agentic AI Security Shifts to Structural Defenses

July 2026 marked a critical turning point for Agentic AI security, shifting focus from theoretical vulnerabilities to structural, systemic defenses. High-profile frameworks from Google DeepMind and Anthropic underscore that AI agents must be treated as potential insider threats, requiring robust, cryptographically verifiable guardrails, strictly scoped identities, and comprehensive runtime monitoring.

## Emerging Threat Landscape

Research has revealed concrete exploitation risks across multiple agent platforms:

- Researchers found an **85% exploitation success rate** when planting attacker instructions in fake Sentry error reports executed by AI coding agents.
- Microsoft details the **AutoJack exploit chain** against AutoGen Studio, which weaponizes an agent's browsing to reach a privileged localhost service and execute code on the host with no user interaction.
- A systematic study of multi-agent systems like MetaGPT and CrewAI found that the **IMA attack achieves 89% success**, highlighting that multi-agent collaboration amplifies harm significantly over single-agent baselines.
